01 / about

Papers in, software out.

Eighteen years of turning cryptography papers into software people can run. I wrote MPC protocols for electricity-market auctions during my PhD, maintained the SCALE-MAMBA framework at KU Leuven, shipped cryptographic services (Apple Pay among them) at Belgium's second-largest bank, and now lead a team at TII building LLM inference on encrypted data. I do my best work where research meets engineering: choosing the protocol that fits, setting security parameters honestly, and getting a small team to deliver it.
